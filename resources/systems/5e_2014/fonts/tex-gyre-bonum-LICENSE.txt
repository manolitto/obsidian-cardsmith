The files in this folder, tex-gyre-bonum-regular.woff2, -bold.woff2,
-italic.woff2 and -bold-italic.woff2, are texgyrebonum-regular.otf,
-bold.otf, -italic.otf and -bolditalic.otf recompressed into the WOFF2
container (fontTools) and renamed. Modifications, as the LPPL asks them
to be identified: the container only. No glyph, metric, name-table or
hinting data was altered; the fonts are TeX Gyre Bonum as released.

The card system's templates and stylesheets only reference these files.
